FREY BOYS / BACKYARD CHALLENGE

TUBE WARS.

Move left and right to build speed and bump rivals. Cut inward across an inner wake line to jump; hit it faster for a bigger launch (an exceptionally hard landing after a maximum-speed jump can occasionally throw the jumper off), and fight for center positions before surprise turns. Go wide for big hits, but dodge logs, buoys, and parked boats. Obstacles knock you off immediately and cost 10 Grip; Dad stops while riders swim back. Riders with Grip remaining swim back to their own tube and continue. Brace recharges slowly. Grip never does. During turns, damage is automatic and cannot eliminate you. The last tube standing wins! Only fully eliminated riders swim to Dad and sit aboard the boat.

Keyboard: P1 A / D · P2 J / L · P3 ← / → · P4 Numpad 4 / 6 (or F / H). Gamepads: left stick / D-pad.

🟦 Brace: regenerating protection🟩 Grip: permanent survival💥 Wake landings: outside-in only • victim 35 • jumper 5🔗 Chain transfers: 65% • contact alone = 0 damage

Runs entirely in this browser. Mobile controls automatically match the selected player count. Landscape recommended. Connect controllers before starting. Esc pauses.
